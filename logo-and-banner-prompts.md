# Logo and SUBSCRIBE banner prompts (same cartoon style as the video)

Replace the character description in prompt 1 with your own design if you already have one.

## 1. Channel logo with your own character (square, 1:1)

```
A square channel logo in 2D cartoon web-comic style: one original character centered, a deadpan character with a plain white round head, simple dot eyes, a tiny flat mouth, thick black outline, short messy brown hair, wearing a dark green zip jacket, shown from the chest up facing the viewer with a calm, slightly skeptical expression, holding a small rolled-up old scroll in one hand to represent history. Clean plain white background, no scenery, bold clean outline, flat colors with soft painterly shading, the character centered with generous empty space around it so it can be cropped into a circle. No text, no letters, no other characters, 1:1.
```

## 2. SUBSCRIBE banner for the YouTube channel header (16:9, black letters on white)

The word must fit inside the "viewable on all devices" safe area: the central 60% of the width and the central 30% of the height.

```
A wide 16:9 banner with a pure solid white background and the single word "SUBSCRIBE" written in bold hand-drawn black capital letters, perfectly spelled S-U-B-S-C-R-I-B-E, thick clean strokes in a casual hand-lettered cartoon style. Composition rules: the whole word is small and centered exactly in the middle of the image, it occupies only about 45% of the image width and only about 12% of the image height, with very large empty white margins around it: at least 27% of the width empty on the left and on the right, and at least 40% of the height empty above and below. Nothing touches or gets near the edges. No other text, no shadows, no gradients, no decorations, no other objects, nothing else on the white background.
```

## 3. Optional: banner with your character (also inside the safe area)

```
A wide 16:9 banner with a pure solid white background. In the exact center of the image, inside a small centered group that occupies only about 50% of the image width and about 25% of the image height: on the left of the group, the same deadpan cartoon character with a plain white round head, simple dot eyes, tiny flat mouth, thick black outline, short messy brown hair and a dark green zip jacket, pointing with one hand toward the right; on the right of the group, the single word "SUBSCRIBE" in bold hand-drawn black capital letters, perfectly spelled S-U-B-S-C-R-I-B-E. Very large empty white margins around the group: at least 25% of the width on the left and right and at least 37% of the height above and below. No other text, no shadows, no gradients, nothing else on the white background.
```

## Tips
- **Keep the character consistent:** generate the logo first, then upload it as a reference image in Flow for prompt 3 and for any future scene that uses your character.
- **Spelling:** if the generator misspells SUBSCRIBE, regenerate or add the word in your editor over a blank white banner (delete the word from the prompt).
- **YouTube channel header:** the image should be at least 2560 x 1440 px (16:9). Everything important must stay inside the "viewable on all devices" area, about 1546 x 423 px in the center (60% of the width, 30% of the height). When you crop in YouTube, make sure the whole word sits inside that inner box.
