# Google Flow cartoon-style shot list: The ENTIRE History of the United States in 10 Minutes

**134 images, synced to the narration of `script-history-of-usa-10-minutes.md`.** Average 4.5 s per image, longest 6.0 s, so the picture changes every 3–7 seconds and the video never goes still.

Style: 2D cartoon web-comic (round white heads, dot eyes, deadpan faces, thick black outlines, painted moody backgrounds), matching the reference screenshots. **The art style stays the same so the video looks like one piece, but every shot has its own cast, written out explicitly** (who appears, gender, age, clothes and hairstyle for the era). This fixes the problem of women or modern clothes appearing where they do not belong: each prompt says exactly who is in the scene and "no women" when the scene was historically all men.

Every prompt is self-contained (style line included). **Short overlays (1–3 words) are written into the prompt** as big hand-drawn letters, like the "100+ YEARS" look; if Flow garbles a word, delete it from the prompt and add the text in your editor. **Long overlays** are not in the prompt: add them in the editor.

## Rhythm rules (follow these when editing)
1. **Never hold an image longer than ~6 s.** Every shot below already has its own duration; if a clip runs long, cut to the next image early.
2. **Always move the camera.** Each shot lists a motion (zoom in, pan, pop-in); alternate them so no two shots in a row move the same way.
3. **First 30 seconds = fastest cuts.** Trim each hook shot to ~3–4 s (cut early and add a quick zoom), then settle into ~4–5 s.
4. **Pattern interrupt every 60–90 s:** a quick whoosh, a color flash, or a bigger overlay on a key date.
5. **Sound design:** a soft *whoosh* on every cut, a *pop* when overlay text appears, a low *boom* on big moments (1776, the Moon landing). Keep slavery, war casualties, and 9/11 quiet and respectful: no sound effects, just soft music.
6. **Music:** build energy through the sections; drop volume under the narration, bring it up on scene changes.
7. **Overlay text:** keep it to 1–4 words (date, name, number). If it is not in the image, pop it in for ~2 s in the editor.
8. **Show before you tell:** the image should appear ~0.3 s before the narrator says the key word.
