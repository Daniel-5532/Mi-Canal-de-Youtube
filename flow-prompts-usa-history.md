## Tips for Flow
- **Order matters:** generate every clip with the same style tail so the video looks like one piece.
- **Variations:** if a clip is blocked or looks off, rewrite the subject more generically (e.g., "a soldier" instead of a named person) and try again.
- **Cost:** each generation uses credits. Test one prompt per scene first, then generate extras only for the ones you keep.
- **Fill the time:** 8-second clips won't cover 10 minutes alone. Mix them with animated maps, stills with slow zooms (Ken Burns), and public-domain archive images (Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons).
- **Label AI content:** when you upload to YouTube, mark the video as containing AI-generated or altered content.
